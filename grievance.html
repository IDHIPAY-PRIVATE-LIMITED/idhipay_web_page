Level 4 — RBI Ombudsman

If your complaint concerns a service covered by the Reserve Bank of India and it has not been resolved, you can approach the RBI Ombudsman under the Reserve Bank – Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2026, which took effect on 1 July 2026 and replaced the 2021 scheme.

  • You must first complain to the regulated entity and wait 30 days for a reply, unless a shorter period applies under other rules.
  • You then have 90 days from the end of that period, or from the entity's final reply, to file.
Onlinecms.rbi.org.in
Emailcrpc@rbi.org.in
By postCentralised Receipt and Processing Centre, Reserve Bank of India, Central Vista, Sector 17, Chandigarh – 160017

IDHIPAY is a technology service provider and is not itself regulated by the Reserve Bank. A complaint to the RBI Ombudsman about a payment is made against the regulated entity that handled it, such as our payment aggregator, Easebuzz, or your bank.

What to include

  • Your merchant ID and registered mobile number.
  • The transaction reference or UTR, the amount and the date.
  • Your earlier ticket number, if you are escalating.
  • What went wrong and what you would like us to do.
  • Screenshots or the bank statement entry, if they help.

Complaints about your data

For anything about your personal data — access, correction, erasure or consent — see our privacy policy and write to the Grievance Officer above. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Notes

  • Complaints about the goods or services a shop sold are between the customer and that shop. We can help confirm whether a payment reached the merchant.
  • We never ask for your UPI PIN, card PIN, CVV or an OTP. Nobody from IDHIPAY will ever ask for them.
