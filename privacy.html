This policy explains how IDHIPAY PRIVATE LIMITED ("IDHIPAY", "we", "us") handles personal data in the IDHIPAY Business app for Android and iPhone (package and bundle ID in.idhipay.merchant) and the services behind it. It is written to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the "DPDP Act") and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025.

The short version

  • We collect only what we need to sign you in, show your UPI QR and tell you about payments you receive.
  • We never receive, hold or move your money. Payments are processed by our payment aggregator partner, Easebuzz, and settled by it straight to your bank account.
  • Your data is stored in India. We do not sell it, show ads or use analytics or tracking tools.
  • You can ask us to delete your account in the app, on our account deletion page, or by emailing support@idhipay.com.
  • Some records must be kept by law, such as payment records for 8 years. Section 7 says what we keep and for how long.

1. Who we are

IDHIPAY PRIVATE LIMITED, a company in India, makes the IDHIPAY Business app. For the personal data described in this policy, IDHIPAY is the Data Fiduciary under the DPDP Act: we decide why and how it is used.

IDHIPAY is a technology service. We never receive, hold, pool or move merchants' or customers' money, and no IDHIPAY bank account is part of any payment or settlement.

2. Who this policy covers

  • Merchants: businesses that IDHIPAY has registered, and the people who sign in to the app for them. Accounts cannot be created in the app; IDHIPAY sets each one up.
  • Customers who pay a merchant ("payers") by scanning the merchant's QR with a UPI app. Payers do not use our app. We receive a few of their details from the payment aggregator when they pay (section 3.4).

3.4 Payers

When a customer pays a merchant's QR, the payment aggregator sends us the payment's details, which can include the payer's UPI ID and name. We keep them encrypted and use them only to record and reconcile the payment, and to look into fraud, disputes and legal requests. We do not show them in the merchant app or use them for anything else.

3.5 What we do not collect

The app does not access your location, contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or SMS inbox. Your phone's keyboard may offer to fill in the sign-in code from the SMS; the app itself does not read your messages. The app speaks payment amounts using your phone's own text-to-speech engine. When you share your QR card, the image is made on your phone and passed to the app you choose; we do not upload it. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

4. How we use data

We use personal data only to:

  • provide the app and your account, and show and announce your payments;
  • sign you in and keep your account secure;
  • keep the financial, audit and security records the law requires;
  • prevent and investigate fraud and abuse;
  • answer your requests and support questions; and
  • meet our legal and regulatory duties.

We use your data because you asked us to provide the service, and for the legitimate uses and legal duties the DPDP Act allows. We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising or build marketing profiles.

6. Where data is stored

We store your data in India, on Amazon Web Services in Mumbai, with backup copies in Hyderabad. Payment data stays in India, as the Reserve Bank of India's directions on storage of payment system data require. The only transfers outside India are the app checks described in section 5 (Google Play Integrity on Android and Apple App Attest on iPhone), which carry no payment data.

8. How we protect data

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Our database and backups are encrypted at rest. Mobile numbers and payers' names and UPI IDs are also encrypted field by field.
  • Sign-in codes and tokens are stored only as one-way hashes. Tokens on your phone are kept in its secure storage, protected by the Android Keystore or the iPhone Keychain.
  • Sign-in is checked with Google Play Integrity on Android and Apple App Attest on iPhone, and repeated attempts are limited.
  • Access to our systems is restricted, needs multi-factor sign-in, and is logged. Sensitive admin actions need a second person to approve them.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach happens, we will tell the people affected and the Data Protection Board of India, as the DPDP Rules require.

9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • get a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it, and the names of those we have shared it with;
  • ask us to correct, complete or update it;
  • ask us to erase it, subject to the records we must keep (section 7);
  • withdraw consent, where we rely on your consent;
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or cannot act yourself; and
  • raise a grievance with us (section 13), and then, if you are not satisfied, with the Data Protection Board of India.

To make a request, email support@idhipay.com with your business name and the last 4 digits of your registered mobile number. Before we act, we confirm that the request comes from the account holder, by a call or a code to the registered number.

10. Deleting your account

You can ask us to delete your account in either of these ways:

  1. In the app: open Account, tap Delete account, then confirm.
  2. Without the app: follow the steps on our account deletion page, or email support@idhipay.com with the subject "Delete my account", your business name and the last 4 digits of your registered mobile number. We confirm the request by a call or a code to that number.

What happens next:

  • When you ask: you are signed out on every phone at once.
  • Within 30 days: we process your request. Your account is suspended, your QR codes are disabled, and your sign-in records are deleted.
  • 90 days after processing: we erase or anonymise your mobile number, names and contact details, unless a legal hold applies.
  • What we keep: payment records (with your business name on them and on your QR codes) for 8 years, and security logs for 180 days (section 7).

Your account with Easebuzz is separate. Tell us in your request if you also want it closed and we will pass that on, or contact Easebuzz yourself.

11. Children

IDHIPAY Business is for businesses and is meant only for adults. It is not for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect children's personal data. If you believe a child's data has reached us, email support@idhipay.com and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will post the new version on this page with a new effective date. If a change materially affects how we use your data, we will tell merchants before it takes effect, using the contact details we hold.
