About us

We build the payment tools that sit on a shop counter: a QR code, a speaker that calls out every payment, a card machine and an app that keeps track of it all.

What we do

IDHIPAY gives shops and small businesses one place to take money and keep track of it.

  • Take payments

    A UPI QR code that works with every UPI app, a card machine that also takes UPI, and payment links for customers who are not at your counter.

  • Hear every payment

    Our speaker reads out the amount as it arrives, in the language the shopkeeper chooses, so nobody has to check a phone mid-rush.

  • Keep track

    Every payment, from the counter and online, lands in one app with daily totals and a clear record of what has settled to the bank.

  • Grow

    A payment history on IDHIPAY can help our lending partners make a working capital offer that is repaid from daily collections.

Our story

IDHIPAY was founded in 2026 in Kolkata, West Bengal, as IDHIPAY PRIVATE LIMITED.

Most small shops now take their money through UPI, but checking every payment on a customer’s phone slows down a busy counter and leaves room for mistakes. We set out to give every shop a QR code that works with any UPI app, and to tell the shopkeeper the moment a payment is confirmed.

Today our merchant app, IDHIPAY Business, is available on Google Play. Payments to our merchants’ QR codes are processed and settled straight to their bank accounts by our payment aggregator partner, Easebuzz.

What we care about

  • Plain language

    Fees, settlement and terms explained the way a shopkeeper would explain them, in the languages our merchants actually speak.

  • Money that arrives

    Collections settle to the bank account the merchant verified, and every payment is visible in the app as it happens.

  • Built for a busy counter

    Hardware and software that work when it is loud, crowded and the queue is long.
