How a payment reaches you

From your customer's phone to your bank account, in four steps.

  1. 1

    Your customer scans the QR

    Any UPI app can read it, so nobody has to download anything new.

  2. 2

    They approve with their UPI PIN

    The amount leaves their bank account the moment they confirm.

  3. 3

    Your speaker reads it out

    You hear the amount straight away, and the payment appears in your app.

  4. 4

    The money settles to your bank

    Collections go to the bank account you verified when you signed up.
